tagline: A receive-only email inbox for AI agents, on your own domain and Cloudflare's free tier.
description: 'A receive-only email inbox for AI agents. Cloudflare Email Routing hands each email to a small Worker that saves it to an R2 bucket as a raw .eml file, and a Python reader with no dependencies lists, parses, and exports the mail as JSON for an agent to read. The Worker has no public URL, the reader only gets a read-only key to that one bucket, and optional sender allowlists and DMARC checks keep out mail you did not ask for. I use it to let an agent read my newsletters without giving it my whole inbox.'
